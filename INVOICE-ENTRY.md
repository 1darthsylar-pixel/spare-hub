# Entering invoices into the FCR

**Who this is for:** whoever enters invoices. Today that is the Operator or the
Executive Director. It is written to be read by a person, not by a developer.

**Approved by Matt Jackson, 14 Aug 2026.** Every rule below came from him and is
here because getting it wrong has a cost.

---

## The six rules

1. **CFA Supply's system is wrong. Work around it, do not trust it.**
2. **Food and paper come off the Overview screen. Never the daily view.**
3. **Credits come off the Overview screen too.**
4. **Produce is not in CFA Supply. Produce is only in Signal.**
5. **Outgoing transfers get a minus sign in front of the dollar amount.**
6. **Copy and paste everything you can. Anything from CFA Supply or CFA Home
   has to be hand typed.**

---

## Why rule 2 exists

The daily view dates each invoice by the **night order**, not the delivery.

A real example from Gate City: invoice 3752901 reads Aug 13 in the daily list.
The food landed Friday Aug 14. Every invoice in that list is dated a day before
the food actually arrived.

Add up eleven daily rows at month end and you have pulled a day's food into the
wrong month. The Overview screen is a monthly total, so the offset cannot reach
you.

## Why rule 4 exists

**Produce lives in Signal.** That is where you get the number.

⚠️ **DO NOT EXPLAIN THIS BY COMPARING IT TO CFA SUPPLY.** An earlier version of
this file did, and Matt's answer on 14 Aug 2026 was that the produce rule "has
nothing to do with CFA Supply". Framing it as a warning about the Supply app
turned a one-line fact into something a reader has to untangle. Produce is its
own number in its own place, and that is the whole rule.

## Why rule 5 exists

A transfer out is food leaving the building. Enter it as a positive number and
you add it to your cost twice: once when you bought it, and again when you gave
it away.

## Why rule 6 exists

CFA Home shows its reports in a viewer. You can see the figure but you cannot
lift it cleanly, so you read it and type it. Same with CFA Supply. Everywhere
else, paste it, because typing is where digits get dropped.

---

## What the Overview screen gives you

One monthly figure per category. That is the shape the FCR wants:

- Food
- Paper
- Cleaning Supplies
- Customer Service Amenities
- Distribution Fee
- Credits

The daily view gives you one category at a time, day by day. Eleven rows to add
up by hand, and a whole category is easy to miss.

---

## One thing that is not a rule

InFORM sometimes shows a banner saying the Discounts and Giveaways report has a
data issue.

If you open it and Discounts shows **$0.00 food cost** against a real net
figure, that is the known issue and not something you did. The Giveaways half
still computes normally.
